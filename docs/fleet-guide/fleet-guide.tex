The live test posts three kinds of job to Research Fleet. For the result, read the daily
record, \job{fwdtest-daily}, which carries the main result: settled net P\&L per strategy. The full picture is on the
\href{https://curiousfun88.github.io/event-pred-live/}{live dashboard}.

\section*{The three jobs}

Each job's name says which kind it is. Open a job and choose Result to see its metrics. Jobs are posted to the home
site, with office as the backup. Hourly jobs from 18 to 21 October 2026 are on the office site, so switch the site
selector to office to see those.

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}P{4.3cm} P{3.3cm} L P{2.9cm}@{}}
\toprule
Job name & Posted & What it shows & Read it for \\
\midrule
\job{fwdtest-daily-}\newline\job{YYYYMMDD} & Once a day, about 02:00 UTC, for the previous day
  & The main result: settled net P\&L and return per strategy & \textbf{The result} \\
\job{fwdtest-pnl-}\newline\job{YYYYMMDDTHH} & Every hour, one per ledger entry
  & The main result's settled net P\&L per strategy at that hour & Tracking the result through the day \\
\job{fwdtest-hour-}\newline\job{YYYYMMDDTHH} & Every hour
  & What the strategy decided that hour: markets scanned, quotes considered, share posted
  & Checking the system runs, not the result \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\section*{Daily record (\job{fwdtest-daily})}

The metrics are the main result: G2 and five baselines on the 23 series G2 was trained and backtested on, counting
quoting hours from 16 October 2026 17:00 UTC. Every figure is settled positions only, with pessimistic fills, net of
Kalshi's fees.

\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}P{5.6cm} L@{}}
\toprule
Metric & Meaning \\
\midrule
\job{main\_g2\_net\_usd} & G2's settled net P\&L, in US dollars \\
\job{main\_g2\_ret\_pct} & G2's net P\&L as a percentage of the capital its settled positions committed \\
\job{main\_g2\_minus\_rnd2\_usd} & G2's net P\&L minus that of random quoting at G2's posting share: the headline
  comparison \\
\job{main\_<code>\_net\_usd}, \job{main\_<code>\_ret\_pct} & The same two figures for each baseline (codes below) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

As of 21 October 2026 01:00 UTC, for example, G2 had +\$115.23 (+7.3\%) over 40 settled events, against
$-$\$181.59 ($-$6.7\%) for random quoting at the same share: G2 minus random +\$296.82. A strategy with no settled
position yet has no metric. The job's output also carries the full table, net P\&L by day, and the hash of the
latest ledger entry, which ties the record to the hourly audit trail. In \job{settled\_net\_usd\_by\_day} each value
is the running total at the end of that day, not the day's own P\&L.

\section*{Hourly P\&L (\job{fwdtest-pnl})}

One job per hourly ledger entry. Its metrics are the main result's settled net P\&L at that hour, with the same names
as the daily record. Each strategy has \job{main\_<code>\_net\_usd}, and the headline comparison is
\job{main\_g2\_minus\_rnd2\_usd}. The hour in
the job name is the quoting hour. The job is posted a few hours later, once that hour's trades have been fetched and
matched.

Jobs posted before 20 October 2026 20:41 UTC use an older format, which also lists the full universe of markets,
positions still open, and test statistics on the full universe. In those, read only the names with
\job{\_main\_realised\_usd}: \job{g2\_main\_realised\_usd} is the same figure as \job{main\_g2\_net\_usd}.

The output holds the ledger entry's hash and the previous entry's hash, so each post can be checked against the
public ledger.

\section*{What is final and what is not}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=3pt]
  \item \textbf{Settled positions are final.} A position counts once every contract in its event has settled at \$1
  or \$0. Positions still open are left out of every figure above, so the totals step up or down as events settle.
  \item \textbf{The verdict comes at the cuts.} The pre-registered tests are computed on settled events at the
  interim cut (28 October 2026, positions settled by 30 October) and the final cut (11 November 2026). Until then the
  figures are provisional and can move a lot.
  \item \textbf{Job ID prefixes name the Lumid site that accepted the post} (\job{home:} or \job{office:}), not where
  the strategy runs. A post that a site rejects is re-sent within the hour, so a missing hour usually appears later.
  Some hours then appear twice, with identical figures.
  \item \textbf{The secondary result} (the full universe of markets, 15 to 17 October) is not in these jobs. It is a
  single line on the dashboard and is reported in the cut report.
\end{itemize}

The rules, dated amendments and host log are in the
\href{https://github.com/curiousfun88/event-pred/blob/fix/post-close-carry-forward/docs/planning/lumid-forward-test-preregistration.md}{pre-registration}.
